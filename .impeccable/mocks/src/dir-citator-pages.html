About the data

Every transcript here comes from the New York State Senate's Open Legislation service. We split each transcript into speakers, match them to senators, and link the bills read on the floor. We never change the words.

New and corrected transcripts are checked every hour. Page numbers are the official transcript pages, so every citation can be checked against the original.

Known gaps

  • Assembly transcripts aren't published by the source, so only the Senate is covered.
  • Full bill details are available from 2009 onward. Older debates show the bill number only.
  • A small number of older transcripts are shown as plain text because their speakers couldn't be separated reliably.
