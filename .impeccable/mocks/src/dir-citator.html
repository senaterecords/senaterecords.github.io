Regular Session · Tuesday, June 10, 2025 · Albany, New York · pp. 5775–5838
No. 412 · S4597-ARelates to the central business district tolling program
5780

The SecretaryCalendar Number 412, by Senator Ortega, Senate Print 4597-A, an act to amend the vehicle and traffic law, in relation to the central business district tolling program.

Acting President BrennanSenator Ortega on the bill.

Senator OrtegaThank you, Mr. President. This bill does one thing. It says the congestion pricing program has to answer to the riders in my district before it answers to anyone else in this building.

*5781

It requires the authority to publish, every quarter, where each dollar collected in the zone is spent, and it gives this body the power to call them in when they don't.

Senator KearneyWill the sponsor yield?

Acting President BrennanDoes the sponsor yield?

Senator OrtegaI do.
