About

On the Record makes New York State Senate transcripts easy to search, read and cite. Find what any senator said on the floor or in a public hearing, follow a bill's debate, and quote it with the official page number.

Where the data comes from

Transcripts, bills and member details come from the New York State Senate's Open Legislation API. The site checks for new transcripts every hour.

Citing a transcript

Page numbers match the official stenographic record, so every quote can be checked against the original.
